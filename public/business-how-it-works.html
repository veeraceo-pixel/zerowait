How skipQs works for businesses

From walk-ins to predictable, digital queues in a few simple steps.

1. Create your account

Sign up, add your location, and set your opening hours.

2. Add your services

Define services, average duration, and queue rules.

3. Share your link or QR

Customers can join the queue from their phone or on-site via QR code.

4. Manage queues in real time

See who's waiting, mark customers as served, and pause queues when needed.

5. Learn from analytics

Understand peak times, average waits, and staff performance.
