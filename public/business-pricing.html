Frequently asked questions

Is it really free — no catch?

Yes. We're in launch mode and our priority is getting real businesses on board and proving the product works. You get everything for free until we announce paid plans, with plenty of notice before that happens.

When will paid plans launch?

We don't have a date yet. We'll only introduce paid plans once we have happy customers and a strong reason to. Early adopters will always get preferential rates.

Do patients pay to use skipQs?

Never. Patients always use skipQs completely free. They just open a link or scan a QR code — no account, no app, no payment.

What type of business can register?

Any business where people queue — hospitals, clinics, salons, barbers, restaurants, banks, government offices, repair shops, gyms, pharmacies. If you have waiting customers, skipQs is for you.

How long does it take to go live?

Under 5 minutes. Register, add your departments, share your link or QR code. That's it — patients can start joining your queue immediately.

What data does skipQs store?

Only what's needed to run the queue: business name, department wait times, and queue counts. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy.
