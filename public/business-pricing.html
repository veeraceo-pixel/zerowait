Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan really free?

Yes, the Starter plan is completely free — no credit card, no time limit. You can register your business and go live in minutes with up to 5 departments.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no contracts or lock-in periods. Cancel your Growth subscription at any time from your dashboard and you won't be charged again.

Do patients pay to use skipQs?

No. Patients always use skipQs for free. The platform is funded by provider subscriptions, not by the people waiting in queues.

What is the bulk CSV import?

Growth plan providers can upload a CSV file (or paste a list) of their departments or services. skipQs parses them instantly and lets you set wait times before saving — ideal for hospitals with 20+ departments.

What data does skipQs store?

Only what's needed to run the queue: business name, department wait times, and anonymous queue counts. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy.

Does skipQs need an internet connection?

Yes. skipQs is a real-time web app built on Supabase. All providers and patients need an internet connection for live updates to work.

Ready to cut wait times?

Join hundreds of providers already using skipQs to keep patients informed and reduce walk-outs.
