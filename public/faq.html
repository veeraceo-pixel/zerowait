Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions from customers and businesses.

What is skipQs?

skipQs lets you join queues remotely for salons, clinics, banks and more — from your phone.

Is skipQs free for customers?

Yes. Joining and tracking queues as a customer is completely free.

How do businesses use skipQs?

Businesses create an account, add their services, and manage queues from the provider dashboard.

Do I lose my place if I'm late?

Each business sets its own grace period. You'll see the rules before joining a queue.

Can I cancel a queue?

Yes, you can cancel from the "My queues" page if you no longer need the service.

How much does it cost for businesses?

We offer a free tier and paid plans. See our pricing page.
