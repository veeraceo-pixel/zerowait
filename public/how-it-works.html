Patients & Customers

Skip the wait in 5 easy steps

1

Find a service

Search for hospitals, clinics, salons, banks, or any registered service near you. Browse by category or search by name — no account needed to browse.

Hospitals & Businesses

Set up your queue in minutes

No technical knowledge required. If you can send an email, you can manage skipQs.

1

Register — it's free

Create your business account in under 5 minutes. No credit card required. We'll walk you through setup step by step.

2

Add departments or services

Create individual queues for each department (e.g., A&E, Cardiology, Pharmacy) or service type. Set an initial estimated wait time for each.

3

Update wait times in seconds

From your dashboard, adjust wait times with a single tap. Changes are pushed instantly — no page reload, no delay, no confusion.

4

Customers see live times instantly

The moment you update, all customers currently browsing or queued see the new time. Full transparency builds trust and reduces no-shows.

5

Manage your queue in real-time

See who's in queue, call the next patient, mark no-shows, and review analytics — all from one clean dashboard. No training required.

Quick Answers

Common questions

Do I need to download an app?

No. skipQs works entirely in your browser on any device. Visit the site, find your service, and join — no installation required.

How accurate are the wait times?

Wait times are updated directly by staff at each location. The more actively a business manages their dashboard, the more accurate the estimates will be.

What if I miss my turn?

This depends on the service provider's policy. Some allow you to rejoin at the back, others may hold your spot briefly. The hospital or business decides.
