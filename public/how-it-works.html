Patients & Customers

Skip the wait in 5 easy steps

1

Find a service near you

Search for hospitals, clinics, salons, banks, or any registered service. Browse by category or search by name — no account needed to browse.

2

See live wait times per department

Each department shows a live wait time updated in real-time by staff. Choose the right department before you even leave home.

3

Join the virtual queue

Tap "Join Queue" and you're in — remotely, from anywhere. You'll receive your queue position and an estimated wait time instantly.

4

Watch your live countdown

Your personal timer runs on the homepage. Watch your position update in real-time as the queue moves — no refreshing needed.

5

Arrive only when it's your turn

Head to the venue when it's nearly your turn. Walk in, get seen straight away, and leave. No waiting room stress, no wasted time.

Hospitals & Businesses

Set up your queue in minutes

No technical knowledge required. If you can send an email, you can manage skipQs.

1

Register — it's free

Create your business or hospital account in under 5 minutes. No credit card required. We walk you through every step.

2

Add departments or services

Create individual queues for each department (A&E, Cardiology, Pharmacy) or service type. Set estimated wait times for each.

3

Update wait times in seconds

Adjust wait times with a single tap. Changes are pushed live instantly — every patient sees the update immediately.

4

Manage your queue live

See every patient in the queue, call them with one click, or reach them instantly via WhatsApp — no phone-number hunting needed.

5

View stats & grow

See served counts, average wait times, and peak hours in your dashboard — helping you staff better and reduce bottlenecks.

Quick Answers

Common questions

Do I need to download an app?

No. skipQs works entirely in your browser on any device. Visit the site, find your service, and join — no installation required.

How accurate are the wait times?

Wait times are updated directly by staff at each location in real-time. The more actively a business manages their dashboard, the more accurate the estimates.

Is skipQs free to use?

Free for patients and customers — always. Every feature is free for businesses during our launch period, with at least 60 days' notice before any paid plans are introduced. See pricing →
