Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At skipQs, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our virtual queuing platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and phone number when you register for an account or join a queue.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to manage your place in line, notify you of your turn via WhatsApp/SMS, and help business owners identify their customers.

3. Location Data

We use your location to show you nearby businesses with active queues. This data is only used while the app is active and is not stored permanently.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (Google) for data storage and authentication. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@skipqs.com.
