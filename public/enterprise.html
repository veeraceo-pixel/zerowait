Patient arrives at reception

2

Scan → select department → join queue

In under 20 seconds, they're in the virtual queue for their department. No app install required — works on any phone browser.

3

Patient waits anywhere

They can sit in the café, their car, or the garden. Their phone shows a live countdown and buzzes when they're next.

From the skipQs dashboard on a tablet or laptop, reception staff tap "Call Next" and the patient is notified instantly.
