Our Mission

We believe your time is precious

skipQs eliminates pointless waiting by connecting people with real-time queue data. Whether you're heading to a hospital, a salon, or a government office — you deserve to know exactly when to arrive.

We partner directly with service providers to give their customers a seamless, stress-free experience — and give businesses a tool that actually improves throughput and customer satisfaction.

Our Story

Built in the UK, serving globally

skipQs was founded to fix the broken experience of standing in queues. Our founders spent too many hours waiting at NHS hospitals, bank branches, and government offices — watching time disappear with no information and no control.

We built a platform that's easy for businesses to set up in minutes, and even easier for customers to use — no downloads, no friction. Just open a browser, find your service, join the queue, and get on with your day.

Today we work with hospitals, clinics, salons, and local businesses across the UK and beyond — and we're just getting started.
